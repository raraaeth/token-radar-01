How every row is priced

The snapshot's trading protocols give the market's own answer to what a dollar of TVL is worth as token value: their market-cap / TVL ratio. The model takes the median ratio inside each category (a category with fewer than 4 trading members uses the whole-snapshot median instead), and prices a pre-token protocol at TVL × its category multiple. Three bounded adjustments follow, each from the row's own figures: a momentum factor from the 7-day TVL change (clamped to −30%…+60%, divided by 200, so one wild week cannot dominate), a track-record factor that grows logarithmically with the days run with no token (up to +30% at five years), and a reach factor of +2% per extra chain (capped at +20%). No protocol's name is an input. Open any row to see its full breakdown and the trading comparables behind its multiple.
